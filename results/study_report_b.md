subject B the function `section_paper()` carries two checks: the frozen

mechanism 1 therefore removes one of mechanism 6's two checks as well.

2 checks and ablating mechanism 6 drops 3, sharing the em-dash check.

the fact; what is reported instead is that mechanism 1's ablation figure
for subject B is contaminated by mechanism 6 to the extent of one check.
Subject A had no such overlap, because there each mechanism owned its own
module. This is a property of a single-script architecture, not of the
method.
